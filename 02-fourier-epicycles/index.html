WHAT'S HAPPENING?

A complicated drawing is being represented as a sum of simple rotating vectors. More Fourier terms means more frequencies, which means a more accurate reconstruction.

f(t) = Σ cₙ · eⁱⁿᵗ
THE IDEA

Complex shapes are hiding simple waves.

Fourier analysis says that complicated periodic signals can be decomposed into simpler sinusoidal components. Here, each component becomes a rotating vector — an epicycle. Add enough of them together and the endpoint traces the original shape.
