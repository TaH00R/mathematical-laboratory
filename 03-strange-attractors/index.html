The idea

A strange attractor is a pattern traced by a deterministic system that keeps evolving without settling into a simple repeated orbit. Lorenz and Rossler use different coupled differential equations to generate these paths.

Lorenz: dx/dt = sigma(y-x); dy/dt = x(rho-z)-y; dz/dt = xy-beta*z
